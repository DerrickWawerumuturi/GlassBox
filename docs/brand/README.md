# Glassbox brand

Glassbox is job hunt intelligence. Job ads list everything; Glassbox counts what today's jobs ask for and shows people where they stand. The core idea is **counted clarity**. The line is **“We show, you decide.”** Glassbox is for job seekers everywhere, not a regional job board.

## Voice

Be warm, direct and evidence led. Let specific counts create interest; do not use hype or false urgency. Name what every number counts. Say what the data cannot establish. Lead with strengths when comparing a CV with jobs, and never tell a person what to learn or apply for.

## Visual identity

- Warm paper and charcoal grounds; orange marks actions and annotations, not data.
- Green marks skills on a CV. Grey hatching marks skills not yet on it. Lime is a single callout, not a general data colour.
- Space Grotesk is the product display face; Schibsted Grotesk is body text; JetBrains Mono is for labels and numbers; Caveat is a restrained handwritten note. Fonts are self hosted.
- Keep chart titles explicit, axes honest, labels legible and sources close to the visual. Add a readable text or table equivalent. The chart grammar and current CSS tokens are defined in [the design system decision](../decisions/design-system.md).
- The approved logo variants and usage rules are in the [logo asset notes](assets/README.md).

## References and boundaries

The chart work studied **Search Party** for subject-first titles, an orange source slash, framed plots, short ticks and restrained highlighting. Glassbox adapted those devices to its own data rules: orange is not a data colour; green means on the CV; hatching means not yet; counts and table equivalents stay visible. The current grammar is in the [design system decision](../decisions/design-system.md); older chart mockups are local reference material, not current data.

For visual investigations, **The Pudding** is a process and craft reference, not a template. Keep the finding and evidence specific to each story. See the annotated [editorial reference library](../editorial/REFERENCE_LIBRARY.md) and [creative principles](../editorial/CREATIVE_PRINCIPLES.md).

Story-specific art direction may change illustration, composition and palette when it helps explain the evidence. The shared identity, accessible reading, accurate sourcing and product voice remain fixed. Current editorial practice is documented in `docs/editorial/`.
