# Glassbox Editorial

Visual investigations built from the weekly count of technology job ads. This is the current source of truth for editorial principles, visual language and production practice. The visual system was checked against the local implementation on 4 Nov 2026.

## Sources of truth

- [`VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`](VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md): shared identity, type and colour roles, story-specific freedom, provenance, accessibility, motion and current story inventory.
- [`EDITORIAL_VISION.md`](EDITORIAL_VISION.md): publication purpose, audience and relationship to the product.
- [`CREATIVE_PRINCIPLES.md`](CREATIVE_PRINCIPLES.md): standards for evidence-led, distinctive stories.
- [`STORYTELLING_GUIDE.md`](STORYTELLING_GUIDE.md): research through publication workflow.
- [`INTERACTION_GUIDE.md`](INTERACTION_GUIDE.md): interaction, touch, keyboard and motion criteria.
- [`REFERENCE_LIBRARY.md`](REFERENCE_LIBRARY.md): annotated references, including Search Party, Johnny Harris, The Pudding and visual journalism practice.
- [`DESIGN_REVIEW.md`](DESIGN_REVIEW.md): rendered screenshot review rubric.
- [`../brand/README.md`](../brand/README.md): product brand and voice. [`../decisions/design-system.md`](../decisions/design-system.md) and [`../decisions/data-visualization.md`](../decisions/data-visualization.md) retain implementation history and data encoding decisions.

## Story material

- `investigations/<slug>/`: research, creative brief, storyboard and implementation notes for an investigation.
- [`experiments/visual-concepts.md`](experiments/visual-concepts.md): concepts explored and rejected for the two-years investigation.
- `sprints/`: dated art-direction decisions.
- `references/`: Glassbox screenshots and diagrams; third-party artwork is not stored here.

## Working sequence

Read this index, the vision, creative principles and visual language, then the active investigation. Verify the evidence and method before developing materially different concepts. Storyboard and prototype the opening, main visual and phone state; review rendered screenshots at 390px and 1280px against the rubric. Publish only after figures and wording are verified and approved.

Use the shared repository instructions in [`../../AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md). Local prototypes, distribution drafts and captured reviews stay in ignored `docs/local/`; they are working records, not another policy source.
