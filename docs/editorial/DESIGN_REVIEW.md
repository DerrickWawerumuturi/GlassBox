# Design review rubric

Score the **rendered page** from screenshots at 390px and 1280px in both themes. Never score from code or from a list of delivered components. Save the screenshots in `docs/local/shots/` and name them in the review.

| Category | 1 weak | 3 solid | 5 excellent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Originality | A template or a familiar default | Some story specific decisions | A concept clearly shaped by this question |
| Central idea | Hard to describe | Clear, not memorable | Memorable, and inseparable from the evidence |
| Visual hierarchy | Competing elements, no entry point | Readable order | A strong opening, clear emphasis, deliberate rhythm |
| Typography and composition | Default looking or inconsistent | Legible, coherent | Characterful and precise, within the product's sizes |
| Data presentation | Hard to read or misleading | Accurate, understandable | The key comparison graspable at once and rewarding to explore |
| Narrative pacing | Repetitive or flat | Logical | Builds understanding with purposeful reveals; nothing repeated |
| Interaction | Decorative or confusing | Useful, conventional | Deepens understanding; optional; accessible; static fallback |
| Distinctiveness | Could be any site | Some Glassbox identity | Glassbox voice with a story specific visual identity |
| Phone | Broken or cramped | Works | Composed for a phone on purpose |
| Accessibility | Essential content or controls unreachable | Main paths usable | Strong keyboard, screen reader, contrast, motion and text alternatives |
| Data integrity | Missing context or wrong claims | Accurate with caveats | Every number traceable; denominators visible; required/preferred/mentioned/not stated never blurred; no intent claims |
| Product coherence | Disconnected | A clear route to Glassbox | Publication and product feel related; the CV check arrives naturally, once |

## Questions every review answers in writing
- What is the single most memorable visual moment?
- What does the reader understand because of the visual that prose wouldn't give them?
- Is the first screen specific to this story, or could it open any story?
- What copy is unnecessary or repeated?
- Is the hierarchy deliberate at 390px?
- Can every important number be traced to a definition, a denominator and a date?
- Does the page still work with reduced motion, without hover, and with JavaScript off?
- The three weakest design decisions.
- What should be removed, not added?
- Does any line tell the reader what to do next? (Then it fails "we show, you decide".)

## Threshold for a flagship story
No score under 3. At least 4 for central idea, data integrity and phone. A written reason for every score under 4. The founder approves the rendered result before publication. Scores support judgement; they don't replace it.
