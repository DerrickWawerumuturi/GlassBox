# The early-career slice is thinner in remote-flagged software listings

**Draft status: provisional v8 research; not for publication.**

A 3 November snapshot of Glassbox's active software-family pool found 31 early-career title signals among 1,614 remote-flagged listings (1.9%). In the not-remote-flagged group, 335 of 3,856 listings carried an early-career title signal (8.7%).

That is a 6.8 percentage-point gap. The share of early-career title signals was about 4.5 times higher in the not-remote-flagged group.

But the word *remote* needs care. Only 94 of the 1,614 remote-flagged listings explicitly said Remote in their workplace metadata. The other 1,520 had no workplace label. Just one of the 94 explicitly remote-labelled roles had an early-career title signal. Thirty of the 31 remote-flagged early-career roles came from aggregators and had no explicit workplace label.

The source split tells a similar story, with an important caveat. Direct ATS listings had one early-career title signal among 296 remote-flagged roles, compared with 179 among 1,661 not-remote-flagged roles. Aggregators had 30 among 1,318 remote-flagged roles and 154 among 2,157 not-remote-flagged roles. The pattern appears in both groups, but the sources do not all define or label remote work in the same way.

Hybrid and onsite listings also carried more early-career title signals in this snapshot: 26 of 273 hybrid roles (9.5%) and 33 of 293 onsite roles (11.3%). Remote-flagged early-career roles were spread across 29 employers; no employer contributed more than two.

The practical read is not that remote early-career jobs do not exist. It is that the early-career title signal is much thinner in the listings currently marked remote, and that most of the remote flag is not backed by an explicit workplace label. Anyone searching for a first software role should not filter out hybrid and onsite opportunities too early.

**Method:** 5,583 counted active roles after manual scope review, from 16 source families, deduplicated by employer and normalized title with documented employer/title aliases. “Early-career” is a title-only proxy (intern, co-op, graduate, junior and related labels), not a guarantee that the description accepts applicants with no experience. This is a current inventory snapshot, not a count of distinct jobs posted over time.

**Do not publish this v8 version.** The profiler v9 classifier is being expanded to correct additional hardware, aerospace, industrial and customer-implementation roles. The story must be recalculated on v9 after a successful source refresh, including persisted Ashby workplace normalization and a fresh residual title audit.
