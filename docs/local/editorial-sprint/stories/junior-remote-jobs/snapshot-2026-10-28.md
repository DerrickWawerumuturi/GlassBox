## Years-parser discrepancy: rechecked

In the current eligible pool, **109** descriptions contain the exact phrase “2+ years of experience”; **3** corresponding v6 profiles leave years unstated. The three remain Holiday World, LangChain, and Tebi. All three stored profile hashes match the current title/description/level/type fields, so this is not explained by a stale description.

The checked-in section markers do not include “What You'll Have,” “How to Be Successful in This Role,” or “What You'll Bring.” The stop markers include “What We Offer” and “About Us.” This makes a section-boundary bug a plausible explanation: after a stop heading, a later qualifications heading that is not recognized may fail to reopen parsing. This is a code-level diagnosis, not yet a verified deployed-runtime diagnosis. Do not use the years aggregates in the article until regression cases pass against the actual profiling path.

## Editorial decision

Keep **“Where Are the Junior Remote Jobs?”** and the Title Scanner storyboard. Do not claim a trend over time or a causal hiring barrier.
