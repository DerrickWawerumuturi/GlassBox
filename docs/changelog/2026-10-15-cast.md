"This is the direction, love them. Most are straight from the library, not ours,

Review page: `docs/local/people-review.html`. GitHub issue #12.

## What changed

- **The cast** (`frontend/src/components/people/`): seven drawings from Pablo
  Stanley's Open Peeps and Open Doodles (CC0 1.0), one TypeScript module each in
  `art/`, made by the prototype's `export.py` (colours as variables, ids dropped,
  numbers rounded: 15 to 29 KB a drawing). `draw.ts` (`personSvg`) inlines one as
  SVG; `Person.tsx` floats it into the text. `LICENSES.md` and `CC0-1.0.txt` sit
  beside them.
- **Where they stand, beside the sentence each adds to:** on /about, Mateo hauling
  a coffee beside "Both are tiring." and Tomas standing beside "We show where you
  stand"; on /product, Kofi walking beside "Move each one along"; in The lobby, Rosa
  pointing up beside "Going up?" and the jobs "waiting in the lobby"; in Contents may
  vary, Joon climbing out of a box beside "So what is in the box?"; in Peak Python,
  Amina standing under "The ridge almost every job stands on" (a section of words
  alone); in Within eleven jobs, Tomas reading on a chair beside "What they mean for
  you is yours to read." `Chapter` takes one as `person` (before paragraph `at`), on
  the side of the words away from the picture. Pick a side, the CV cards and /market
  have none.
- **No backing:** just the drawing. In light the paper fills take the ground they
  stand on (`--person-ground`: the lobby's cream, Contents may vary's ground, else
  the page background).
- **Dark mode:** an SVG filter turns the drawing into line art in the paper colour.
  It erodes the ink to find solid shapes and keeps every line plus the edge of every
  solid, so hair and trousers become outlines with the dark ground showing through.
  The lime box stays lime. The lobby's inside stays cream in both themes, so Rosa
  keeps the light drawing there.
- **Removed:** `components/stories/doll.ts` (the colourful dolls) and every place
  that used it: Pick a side's dealer, the lift's attendant, the reader beside the
  box, the crane's operator (and the cab window's unused clip), /product's CV card,
  the /market CV picture (the CV now sits centred alone). `Chapter`'s unused
  `character` slot became `person`; `CvClose` lost its `character` prop.
