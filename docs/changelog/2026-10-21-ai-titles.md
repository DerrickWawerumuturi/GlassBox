# 2026-10-21: the weekly publication counts jobs with AI in the title

GitHub issue #10 (the AI age stories). Decision: `docs/decisions/market-look.md` ("`ai_titles`").
Review page: `docs/local/ai-titles-review.html`.

## The change

- **`look["ai_titles"]`** (`backend/src/jobpool/ai_titles.py`): every job whose title names AI, in every family,
  `non_tech` and `other` too; the families they sit in, with each family's total; early career and internships; and
  nine kinds by what the title says the job is for (research, customers, bounds, teach, product, change, infra, build,
  other), each with up to 8 titles, one per employer. Counted by `snapshot.counted()`, the publication's own rule.
- **Built in `market_look.bodies`**, so only the weekly publication carries it. It reads the rows the Monday run
  already reads: no new query to Neon, no call to Azure or Groq. The publication row grows by about 6 KB.
- Why now: the story "What do all these AI jobs actually do?" (week of 26 Oct) and the weekly AI barometer read it.
  A live check on 21 Oct counted 2,045 jobs with AI in the title; 106 of them research.

## Tests

`test_ai_titles.py`: which titles name AI (and that call centre "Agent" doesn't), the first kind wins, one count per
role and daily fetch only, every family with its total, kinds in order and summing to the total, eight titles a kind
one per employer, and the publication carries it without ad text (fails with the `bodies` line removed). Backend
suite: 739 passed against the local test database, 689 passed and 21 skipped with `DATABASE_URL=""`.
