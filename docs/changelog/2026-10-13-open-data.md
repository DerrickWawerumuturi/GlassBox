# 2026-10-13: an open weekly dataset of the published counts (fixes #7)

Decision: `docs/decisions/open-data.md`. Review page: `docs/local/open-data-review.html`.

- Backend `src/jobpool/open_data.py`: a published week (from `market_publications`)
  becomes `job_types.csv`, `skills.csv` and `experience.csv`, in a folder per week
  plus all weeks files, `README.md` and `datapackage.json`. Only skills named at 10
  or more employers (`market_story.broad`), no skill rows under the 100 rule, years
  only when the week has them, no company names, titles or links.
  `python -m src.jobpool.open_data --out <dir> [--week 2026-10-12 | --all]`.
- `publication_repository.published_weeks`: one row per published week, the one served last.
- `job-pool.yml`: the collect step outputs the week it published; a new step adds the
  published weeks to the public repository `glassbox-job-counts` when `OPEN_DATA_TOKEN`
  is set, and prints one line when it isn't. Off until the founder sets it up.
- Terms of all 16 sources checked: none forbids publishing counts. Arbeitnow and
  We Work Remotely are unclear; ask both before switching the push on.
- Preview from the two published weeks (one read only query of production):
  `docs/local/open-data-preview/`, 10 job type rows, 400 skill rows, 14 years rows,
  every number checked against the published page bodies.
