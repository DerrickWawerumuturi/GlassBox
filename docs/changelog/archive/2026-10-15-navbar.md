# 2026-10-15: the header's Product and Market as plain links

Decision: `docs/decisions/design-system.md` ("The header"). Review page:
`docs/local/nav-review.html`. Screenshots: `docs/local/nav-review/`.

## The problem

The founder: the Product menu's Market item (the dashboard's Market view)
read as the same thing as the Resources menu's Market (the public market
pages). Product is one page with five sections, so a menu in front of it
added a step and nothing else.

## What changed

- **Four plain links** in `Navbar.tsx`: Product (`/product`), Market
  (`/market`), About, FAQ, from one list (`SITE_LINKS`). The sliding pill on
  hover stays.
- **Current page:** `isCurrent` lights a link on its own page and every page
  under it, so `/market/devops` and the stories light Market. The link carries
  `aria-current="page"`. Before, only an exact match lit up.
- **Phone menu:** Dashboard when signed in, Try it out, then the same four
  links with an icon each. The "Resources" label and the /method item are gone.
- **How Glassbox counts** (`/method`) leaves the header. It stays in the footer
  and on `/market`.
- **Removed as unused:** the Product menu (its icon tiles and its desktop Try
  it out), `FEATURES.nav`, `NAV.resources`, `NAV.marketHubLine`, `NAV.method`,
  `NAV.methodLine`, and `components/ui/navigation-menu.tsx`, which only the
  header used. `cta_clicked` `where: header` is no longer sent; `menu` still is.

## Tests

`components/Navbar.test.ts` renders the header (navigation, session and scan
mocked): the four links and their addresses in order, no Resources and no
menu button on the bar, Market current on `/market`, `/market/devops` and a
story, the phone menu's list. All five fail against the old header; with only
the active state reverted to an exact match, the two current page tests fail.
`tsc` clean; vitest 35 files, 297 passed, 1 skipped; `next build` passes
against local services (local API, local site DB, analytics off).
