# 2026-10-07: the CV ask becomes a question

"Add your CV" is now "How many of these are on your CV?" over the skills the
page just showed, with "? of 15" and "Find out". The upload is a sheet that
starts reading the moment a file is chosen, and the answer lands where the
question was asked. Spec: `docs/local/cv-ask-prototype.html` (founder
approved). Decision: `docs/decisions/cv-ask.md`. Review: `docs/local/cv-ask.html`.

## Frontend

- `lib/cv-ask.ts` (new): the ask's words; which skills it asks about (a market
  page's 15 broad skills named most, a job type's 10 most asked, an ad's
  asks); k of N from the scan; the one ask on screen rule; the sheet's states
  and its reading steps; the file check (PDF, 10 MB) and plain error words;
  `runUpload`, the scan's two requests.
- `components/cv-ask/` (new): `AskCard` and `AskInline`, `StickyAsk`
  (`AskLine`, `useAskInView`), `CvSheet`, `CvPicker`, `Chip`, and
  `useCvScan.tsx` (moved from `components/landing/`, now with the sheet, the
  reading steps and "Use your last CV").
- Market pages: `MarketCvAsk` is the ask card under one `MarketAskProvider`
  per page (one scan, one sheet); the title bar shows the sticky line on a
  desktop, `StickyAsk` at the foot of a phone. The dark "See where you stand"
  block is gone.
- Landing: the count card asks "? of the 10 skills {job type} jobs ask for
  most are on your CV." for the job type picked (replacing the after scan
  line); the sticky bar asks about the count's job type or the ad on the glass;
  the closing section's button is "Find out". The sticky bar steps aside
  while any ask is in view, and after a scan.
- The reading steps move only on answers: `/cv/parse` ticks "Read the PDF"
  and "Find your skills" together, `/analyze` ticks "Compare". The bar is
  indeterminate.
- `/dashboard/scan` uses `CvPicker`; `components/ui/FileUpload.tsx` is
  removed. `AddCvButton` serves /product only.
- `lib/look.ts`: `topTenHave` removed (`familyAskSkills` and `askCount`).
- Analytics: `cta_clicked` gains `ask` (`card` or `sticky`) and the landing
  place `count`, both allowlisted. No new event.
- `globals.css`: `.anim-across`, the sliding segment of a wait with no
  measured progress (off with reduced motion).
