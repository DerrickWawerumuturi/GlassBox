# Glassbox working rules

These rules are mandatory for every task. Follow the full standards in [`docs/FOUNDATION.md`](docs/FOUNDATION.md); do not silently waive them for urgency. If instructions conflict or essential context is missing, explain the conflict and ask for direction.

- Conserve tokens and context. Read named/relevant files first; inspect only what the task needs. Avoid broad audits and repeated searches.
- Read applicable nested `AGENTS.md` files; `frontend/AGENTS.md` has framework-specific rules for frontend work.
- Preserve every existing change, including staged, unstaged, ignored and untracked work. Never reset, clean, overwrite, stage, stash, commit, merge, rebase or push without explicit authorization for that operation.
- Never expose or read `.env` contents, credentials, CVs, personal data or sensitive headers. Treat production data and systems as read-only unless the user explicitly authorizes a specific write.
- Keep changes scoped. Verify behavior with focused checks; never claim success without evidence. Distinguish local, tested, committed, pushed and deployed states.
- Glassbox is evidence-led: “We show, you decide.” Preserve denominators, sources, dates and uncertainty. Keep required, preferred, mentioned and unstated distinct.
- Preserve Glassbox’s existing identity and artwork. Editorial visuals must explain evidence, use licensed or human-created final artwork, and remain accessible and responsive. Do not copy reference designs.
- Communicate concisely. Report blockers early and stop when acceptance criteria are met.
